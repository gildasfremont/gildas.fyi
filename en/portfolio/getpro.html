Portfolio · GetPro, March to June 2026

Repositioning a recruitment firm towards executives, by prototyping the website with the firm's real data

Sixty-nine client cases and ten years of placements as raw material, a website rebuilt page by page, put online almost as is

March and April 2026, then the launch · one page on AI expertise, then the brand repositioning and the content overhaul · the live site

GetPro is a headhunting firm for Tech and AI, founded in 2015, with a team of recruiters and four thousand people placed in ten years. In executive search, fees are justified by visible credibility: the website is the first place where an executive decides whether the firm speaks his language. The initial request was a page on artificial intelligence expertise. The work became a repositioning.

The move

Before
  • Headhunting for Tech, in volume
  • A front-and-centre AI page, as requested
  • A website that lists services
After
  • Executive search CEO, COO, CRO, VP, Head of
  • Volume absorbed by on-demand recruiters
  • Fractional executives, kept separate
  • AI diffuse, one criterion among others, with no dedicated page

You do not speak about the trade of the people you recruit in their place.

The AI page requested at the start does not exist in the delivered site. The angle kept is that the executives the firm places have made AI part of their practice, said once, without naming tools. Three offers replace the list of services: executives on demand, recruiters on demand, headhunting.

Prototyping with real data

Rather than mockups, the site was built directly as pages, with Claude, on the firm's data: sixty-nine client cases with their testimonial and their number of placements, the logos, the team photos, the reviews. Each page was rewritten until the argument held, five hundred and thirty-one commits between April and June.

69client cases with a testimonial, in a single data file.
584placements attributed to those cases, displayed on the site.
531commits, April to June 2026.

The prototype and the site

The live site follows the prototype almost line for line: the title, the team wall, the Mistral and Lucca cases, the three offers. It is the easiest case in this portfolio to check: the prototype is still online, so is the site, and they can be compared.

I have no measure of what the repositioning changed for the firm. The photos and testimonials are those published by GetPro on its site.
