Portfolio · Lucca, 2023 and 2025

The menu of an eighteen-product HR suite, treated as a system rather than a list

Six zones, three states, and what stays visible when a customer uses only three products

Menu and sitemap in the design system in 2023, app switcher in June 2025 · fractional product director at Lucca

Lucca sells a suite. A customer does not buy eighteen products at once; they add them over the years, and each addition makes the menu longer. The navigation is the only place where the whole suite is seen at a glance, by the user looking for their product and by the salesperson who wants to sell one more. Every product team defends its own entry.

18solutions in one column, from the home page to the payroll assistant, with notification badges on six of them.

Six zones

I split the menu into zones, each with a role, before drawing a single state.

  • ControlThe logo, and the button that collapses or expands the menu.
  • Search and sortSearch across Lucca, from wherever you are.
  • NotificationsThe badges, carried by the solutions that have them.
  • Options and solutionsThe list of products, and each one's place in the whole.
  • General settingsPlatform and settings, kept apart from each solution's own settings.
  • ProfileThe signed-in user, at the bottom.

Three states

The three states come from the original board, annotated zone by zone. The menu is a design system component, so every solution inherits it without redrawing it: a new product's place is decided once, where every team can see it.

Two passes, two years apart

The menu and the sitemap were done in 2023, as part of the design system. The app switcher, moving from one solution to another from the collapsed state, came in June 2025. In between, the suite had grown, and the question had stayed the same: which of the eighteen solutions deserves to be visible without a click when a customer uses only three of them.

What we do not know

No measured effect. The menu is in production at Lucca; the screens on this page are from the working board, not captures of the live instance.
