Portfolio · Ornikar, 2014 to 2016

Registering driving licence candidates by hand in ninety-six prefectures, to learn the rules before coding them

Registering for the test as an independent candidate, in three stages, and from zero to 15,000 paying users in eighteen months

First product manager, employee no. 8 · 2014 to 2016 · student and instructor journeys, product team and support team built

In 2014, Ornikar sold driving licences without a driving school licence: the commission turned down every application, the unions took it to court, and the licence only came in 2016 with the Macron law. An Ornikar student learns the theory online, takes lessons with an independent instructor and sits the test as an independent candidate. So they have to be registered with the driving licence office of their département. There are ninety-six of them, each with its own forms, its own lead times and its own reading of the same texts, which lived in the practice of the counter staff and nowhere we could read them.

A startup that has just raised has an implicit brief, the acquisition curve. The problem I saw in the tickets was elsewhere: a student who has paid and cannot get registered asks for a refund and leaves a bad review. Every cohort produced some, each more costly than a student never acquired. I put the product effort on registration.

Three stages, in this order

1By hand
Student→Support team→96 offices

An in-house support team takes every registration and carries it to the right office. Each file teaches one more rule. Not a line of code until the real rules are known.

2Automate
Student→Ornikar→96 offices

When support has no more surprises on a département, what it has learnt becomes code. Automating earlier would have encoded assumptions. The rest still goes through support, which is left with only the special cases.

3Remove the friction
Student→Ornikar→96 offices

Forms, documents, messages, redesigned without assuming anything about the person on the other side. Every step removed on the student's side is a step support had first done by hand.

The order mattered, because each phase produced the knowledge the next one needed. Support was not a cost centre, it was the product's learning organ, and I had to defend it as such in front of founders and investors who expected a curve rather than a team.

The rule that decided the order

Until a rule has been verified on real files, it does not go into the product.

Every screen designed on an assumption about the student, a computer rather than a phone, an easy reading of administrative French, papers in order, broke on the first hundred users. The product addressed the whole population that takes the driving test: no assumption was allowed about equipment, ease with written language or the validity of papers.

What came of it

15,000paying users in eighteen months, starting from zero.
2015Strategic Design Trophy, service design category.
