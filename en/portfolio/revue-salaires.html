Portfolio · Lucca, 2023 and 2024

A salary review module: twenty views requested by real customers, three kept, one per moment of the campaign

The salary review module of Pagga Rémunération, shipped in September 2024, now the sales team's demo

Fractional product director at Lucca · with the head of the suite, the designer and the engineers · product definition and deciding what ships

Once a year, sometimes twice, a company decides who gets a raise and how much. Managers make proposals for their team within a budget envelope, HR and finance consolidate against the budget, the pay policy and fairness, an approval chain closes the campaign, and payroll applies the result. Every company runs it a little differently, by envelope or by hierarchy, in one round or several, and most did it in spreadsheets passed around by email. Lucca wanted a module.

20→3candidate views at the start of design, each requested by a real customer for a real reason, and three kept at release.

One view per moment, not per request

A view per manager, one per envelope, one for the arbitration meeting, one for the person who checks the totals: none of the twenty was wrong, and each one built on its own would have made a reasonable feature. The question was not which was most requested, but what a manager can learn in a single campaign, and what HR needs to read at a glance when the proposals come in. A campaign has few moments.

  • The manager proposes, within their envelope.one view
  • HR and finance consolidate, against the budget.one view
  • Arbitration.the same view, filtered
  • Approval, and hand-over to payroll.one view, and the export
Sort by who asked for itRewards the loudest customer, and produces the same sprawl, later.
Sort by the moment it servesArranged by moment, the twenty requests almost all fitted into three views. The rest goes into a filter, an export, or a module that already exists.

The alternative was to ship all twenty behind a configuration layer. It shifts the decision to a customer who has less context than we do, and it is paid for three times: at build, at set-up, in use. The cost of the opposite choice: seventeen requests without a dedicated screen, to be explained one by one to the customers who had made them, and a refusal to defend before the head of the suite at every new good reason to widen the scope.

What came of it

The module shipped in Pagga Rémunération on 16 September 2024, connected to the employee file, the permissions model and the suite's approval chain, with the export to payroll.

Its first campaign saved two days of meetings with the management line and one day of data centralisation.

The International Office for Water, testimonial published on Lucca's website.

It also became the sales team's demo, though that was not the goal: a model that can be understood in one campaign can also be understood in a fifteen-minute meeting. Building a product on a mature suite is mostly subtraction, and subtraction is harder to defend than addition because every view removed has a customer behind it.

The module's mock-ups belong to Lucca and are not in my files: this page has no screen.
