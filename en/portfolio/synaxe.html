Portfolio · Synaxe, December 2023 to August 2024

For quarries, from weighbridge ticket to invoice: a sensor product repositioned as a compliance tool, and the chain delivered with two developers

Dune, the Synaxe suite for quarries and concrete plants

An audit, then eight invoices over nine months · fractional CPO · interviews, data model, mockups, specifications, delivery

Synaxe publishes Dune, software for operators of quarries, concrete plants and asphalt plants. It was sold as a layer over sensors on the trucks: knowing what the truck carries and where it is. The operators I interviewed talked about something else. They move materials, waste among them, under obligations that keep piling up, and their difficulty was not measuring what they do but proving it afterwards. A site that cannot file its declarations can no longer receive anything. Compliance has a budget line at the customer's; better figures do not.

Five roles, one purchase order passing between them

  • Is the price known at the time of the order? If not, at the time of delivery?
  • Are invoices validated by the sales team?

These two questions separated the contract from the order. The contract is a customer's price list over a period; the order is what has to be delivered. The price may not be known until the invoice.

The model

Customer→Contract→Delivery call-off→Trip, weighings→Delivery→Invoice

The price of haulage, the largest share of the cost of aggregates, depends on the day, the distance, access to the site and the number of round trips the truck makes. Impossible to calculate correctly. The answer we settled on, in the file's comments with the developer: a field filled in at the order, editable at the invoice, and the calculation left to the sales team.

Waste compliance

The second tool tracks the waste entering a site: the prior acceptance declaration, with the producer, the haulier, the origin and the destination, and the two ways of signing it, by hand or electronically. The file contains the captured government online service, the form rebuilt field by field and the signature circuit. I am not showing these screens: they reproduce an administrative form.

What came of it

Both tools are in production at customers, including groups in the sector, and have brought in new contracts and renewals. The repositioning was the founders' decision, which I argued for from the interviews. What counted was less the presentation of the repositioning than the two tools that were running at the end.
